The name

The calendar

We drop on the new moon.

Amavasya is the darkest night of the lunar month. It comes every twenty-nine and a half days and nobody decides when — not even us. Every drop opens at the same astronomical instant everywhere on earth.

The mark is a hand on a dial. It turns thirty degrees a drop, so the piece you own says which night it came from without a number printed anywhere. Hover a dial for its date.
